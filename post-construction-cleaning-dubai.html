What is post-construction cleaning?

Post-construction cleaning, sometimes called a builders' clean or after-renovation cleaning, is the clean that happens once the building work is finished. Construction and fit-out leave fine dust on surfaces and in corners throughout a building. The aim is to turn a finished site into a space people can move into, open or hand over.

When should post-construction cleaning be done?

Book it for the point when construction is complete but before the space is furnished or occupied. Cleaning earlier means trades may add fresh dust afterwards, and cleaning later means working around furniture, fixtures and people. For commercial projects, tell us your handover or opening date and we will plan the clean around it.

Post-construction cleaning or deep cleaning: which do I need?

If the dust comes from building, fit-out or renovation work, you need post-construction cleaning, which is planned around the site condition and project scope. If the home is lived in and simply needs a thorough reset, a deep clean is the right choice. Moving into a finished home? See move-in cleaning. Not sure? How is a post-construction quote worked out?

Three things set the quote: the size of the villa or building, the condition of the site after the works, and the project scope. A site with dust across several floors takes more work than a single-room fit-out. You agree the quote before the team starts, with no hidden fees and supervision included free.

Getting the site ready for cleaning

  1. Confirm the trades are finished. Snagging and touch-ups create new dust, so book the clean after the last works where you can.
  2. Arrange site access. Share gate, security and parking arrangements, and any site rules the team needs to follow.
  3. Make sure water and power are on. The team needs both to clean properly.
  4. Flag sensitive finishes. New stone, wood or coated surfaces are worth mentioning before the clean.
  5. Share the handover date. It lets us plan the work around your programme.
