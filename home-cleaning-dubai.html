Maid service or deep clean: what is the difference?

A maid service or regular cleaning visit looks after the day-to-day: the rooms you live in, kept tidy and fresh on a schedule that suits you. A deep clean is a one-off, top-to-bottom reset that reaches the corners regular cleaning misses. A good pattern is to start with a deep clean, then keep it up with regular visits.

Getting the most from each visit

  1. Agree a priority list. A short list of must-do rooms or tasks keeps every visit focused.
  2. Keep supplies in one place. If the team uses your products, a single cupboard makes them easy to find.
  3. Tidy away clutter. Clear surfaces let the team spend the visit cleaning, not moving things.
  4. Mention pets and delicate items. It helps the team plan the visit around your household.
  5. Flag changes early. Guests coming, or a room that needs extra time? Tell us before the visit.
