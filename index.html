Before your deep clean: 5 things to prep

A little preparation helps the team spend every minute on cleaning.

  1. Clear surfaces and floors. Put away personal items so the team can reach every corner.
  2. Note your focus areas. Walk through your space and send us anything you want extra attention on.
  3. Share access details. Building entry, parking, and any lift booking or gate pass your community needs.
  4. Mention special care. Tell us about pets, fragile items, or surfaces that need gentle handling.
  5. Choose your materials. Our team brings professional-grade supplies, or we can use yours if you prefer.
